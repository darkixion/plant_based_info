      Referenced

      Figures follow USDA FoodData Central. Amino acids are derived from each food's per-protein profile, so they stay consistent with its protein figure.

      Note about limits

      These are representative values, not lab results for your packet. Cultivar, soil and processing all move them.

      Not medical advice. Nutrient needs vary by age, sex, pregnancy, medication and health status. Daily values shown are general adult reference figures (FDA DV / FAO-WHO patterns) and will not match everyone. Talk to a dietitian or GP before making big dietary changes, and note that vitamin B12 cannot be reliably obtained from unfortified plant foods, so supplementation is standard advice on a vegan diet.
